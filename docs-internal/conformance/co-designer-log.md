# Co-designer session log

Co-designers are people with lived experience (blind, Deaf, disabled, displaced) who test our work. Treat their feedback as requirements, not as bug reports to defend against. Get consent before recording or quoting anyone; record only what they agree to; do not store personal or health details in this repo.

## Before the session

- [ ] Ask the organisers which co-designer is paired with our track and how the Saturday test runs (#3)
- [ ] Working build on a laptop and a phone, with the demo lesson loaded
- [ ] Task script ready (see test-protocol.md section 3)
- [ ] One person facilitates, one takes notes; nobody coaches during the task
